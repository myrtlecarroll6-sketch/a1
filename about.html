The Engineering Philosophy of Haute Horlogerie Calibers

By Master Watchmaker Horologists•WristMechanic Laboratory Standards
Inside the WristMechanic Cleanroom Laboratory: where sub-micron 5-axis CNC micro-milling, hand anglage beveling, and Witschi chronometric telemetry converge.

At WristMechanic, our laboratory is devoted to the micro-mechanical physics, metallurgy, and artisanal finishing of haute horlogerie mechanical calibers. We exist at the intersection of ancestral Swiss craftsmanship and cutting-edge material science—engineering movements that achieve chronometric precision through monocrystalline silicon hairsprings, free-sprung Gyromax balances, and hand-beveled German silver bridges.

From analyzing the tangential impulse mechanics of George Daniels' Co-Axial escapement to hand-filing razor-sharp inward mirror angles (angles rentrants) for the Poinçon de Genève, our monographs and simulation studios demystify the micro-world of mechanical timekeeping.

Our Core Horological Disciplines

1. Escapement Physics & Fluid Dynamics: We research radial impulse geometry, synthetic diamond pallet stones, and anti-magnetic silicon hairsprings to eliminate friction and positional errors.
2. Haute Horlogerie Hand Finishing: We preserve traditional hand anglage (berceau rounded beveling), mirror black polishing (poli noir), and circular graining (perlage).
3. 6-Position Chronometric Regulation: We perform static and dynamic isochronism calibration across six spatial orientations and three temperature gradients compliant with COSC and METAS Master Chronometer standards.
