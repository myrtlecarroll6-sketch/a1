Horological Disclosures & Movement Disclaimer

Effective Date: January 1, 2026•WristMechanic Atelier Governance

1. Educational and Scientific Reference Purpose

The technical monographs, escapement physics models, hairspring metallurgy analyses, and chronometric regulation protocols provided on WristMechanic are designed solely for educational, watchmaking reference, and horological research purposes.

2. Watchmaking Bench Safety and Cleanroom Protocols

Disassembling and assembling high-precision mechanical watch movements requires specialized horological tools, cleanroom environments, and proper eye protection. Mainspring barrels store substantial mechanical potential energy; watchmakers must exercise extreme caution when letting down mainsprings to prevent injury or component damage.

3. Independent Technical Research

WristMechanic is an independent horological research studio. Technical comparisons between escapement systems (e.g., Swiss Lever, Co-Axial, Tourbillon) reflect objective kinematic and physical analysis rather than commercial endorsements.

4. Theoretical Modeling and Environmental Variations

All calculations presented in our interactive Escapement Lab reflect standardized mechanical physics models. Real-world mechanical watch performance will vary based on regional ambient atmospheric pressure, thermal gradients, and synthetic lubricant age.
