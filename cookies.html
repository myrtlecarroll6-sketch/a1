Cookie Policy & Digital Preferences

Effective Date: January 1, 2026•WristMechanic Privacy Standards

1. Principles of Digital Transparency and Privacy

WristMechanic maintains an unwavering commitment to digital precision, minimalist performance, and user privacy. We believe your study of our horological monographs and interactive caliber simulations should remain private and unburdened by commercial ad tracking cookies. This Cookie Policy explains our use of essential technical cookies and local preferences.

2. Strictly Necessary Technical & Session Cookies

Essential technical cookies are required for the secure operation, cryptographic session management, and rendering stability of our platform. These cookies maintain encrypted SSL/TLS session states, verify technical contact submissions against cross-site request forgery, and calculate reading progress percentages across technical monographs. Essential cookies cannot be disabled without impairing platform functionality.

3. Local Storage for Theme and Workbench Configurations

To ensure optimal visual ergonomics during extended reading of our technical horological monographs, WristMechanic utilizes client-side HTML5 Local Storage to remember your preferred interface theme (Dark Caliber vs. Light Dial) and last-used Escapement Lab selections. This configuration data resides strictly within your local browser sandbox and is never transmitted to marketing servers.

4. Anonymized Measurement & Performance Analytics

We deploy privacy-compliant Google Analytics 4 telemetry (Google Tag `G-0LY0HY7L01`) to assess aggregate website loading latencies, device viewport dimensions, and navigational engagement. All visitor IP addresses are automatically pseudonymized at the point of ingestion, ensuring no individual identity is ever associated with website visits.

5. Browser Management and Cookie Controls

Users maintain complete freedom to review, disable, or delete stored cookies and local storage tokens at any time through web browser preferences (supported in Safari, Chrome, Firefox, and Edge). Please note that clearing essential storage may reset your custom theme preferences.
