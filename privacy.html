Privacy Policy & Client Data Stewardship

Effective Date: January 1, 2026•WristMechanic Privacy Standards

1. Commitment to Client Confidentiality and Horological Integrity

At WristMechanic, we maintain rigorous data protection standards for all horological researchers, master watchmakers, collectors, and institutional engineering partners. We strictly observe data minimization principles under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and global engineering governance standards.

2. Scope and Nature of Information Collected

To process technical caliber development requests, coordinate cleanroom consultations, and distribute horological research monographs, we collect essential contact details (such as full name, institutional email address, phone number, and movement technical notes). Device telemetry data, such as pseudonymized IP addresses and browser rendering attributes, is collected solely to maintain secure SSL/TLS connections and optimize interactive simulation performance.

3. Lawful Purpose of Data Processing

All personal and technical data is processed exclusively to address movement engineering inquiries, schedule cleanroom visits, and deliver horological monographs. We never deploy user data for automated profiling, cross-site behavioral advertising, or third-party marketing syndicates.

4. Absolute Prohibition on Commercial Data Sale

WristMechanic enforces an absolute institutional guarantee: we do not sell, lease, rent, or trade client records or technical consultation details to commercial marketing brokers or ad networks. Information is shared solely with essential technical infrastructure providers—such as secure cloud hosting and encrypted email systems—under binding non-disclosure obligations.

5. Cryptographic Security and Data Retention

All digital communications and technical inquiry transmissions are encrypted using modern TLS 1.3 protocols in transit and AES-256 encryption at rest. Client records are stored in secure, access-controlled databases restricted to authorized laboratory directors.

6. Universal User Rights and Contact

Users maintain full legal rights to access, update, export, or request the immediate deletion of their personal records.
